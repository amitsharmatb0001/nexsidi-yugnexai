Build Brewline, a small coffee shop pre-order site. Pages: Home, Menu (drinks and pastries with prices), Order (pick items and a pickup time, save the order), About, Contact. Customers sign up and sign in with email and password and see their own orders on a My Orders page. Warm, simple look; works well on a phone. Decisions: payments are handled in-store at pickup. Checkout requires an account (no guest checkout). One physical store location. No limit on orders per pickup time. Staff mark a specific drink out of stock by hand (per drink, not per ingredient). No-shows are reviewed by staff by hand, with no automatic blocking. Baristas manage incoming pre-orders and the menu from a built-in staff dashboard; staff log in with an account the shop sets up, customers sign up themselves. Design: warm and cozy, earth tones (warm browns, soft creams, terracotta accents), no cold corporate look, no purple gradients.
